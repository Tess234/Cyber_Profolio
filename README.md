# Cyber_Profolio

About Me

I became interested in cybersecurity because of a desire to protect the security and privacy of the general public. Anyone born in the last twenty years has never known a world where social media companies and websites weren’t tracking your every move — where personal data, from location history to cross-site tracking, is constantly up for grabs. That reality shaped my decision to major in computer science, and in the future I hope to work with technologies like AI and blockchain to strengthen everyday people’s privacy by limiting how much access companies and apps have to their personal data. I’m currently a cyber student at SANS, working toward a focus in forensics or network security.

My CTF Experience

Competing in CTFs through Cyber Skyline has been challenging, eye-opening, a confidence-builder, and genuinely fun. Going in, I didn’t expect anything too intense — but it turned out to be both more enjoyable and more educational than I anticipated. My approach to each challenge generally starts with recon and fingerprinting, then moves into researching unfamiliar formats and collaborating with teammates. The biggest growth area for me has been building patience with unfamiliar tools and learning to debug methodically rather than jumping to conclusions.

Highlights

- Worked through a multi-format cryptography challenge involving three separately encrypted files, each using a different encryption scheme and key-derivation function. 
- Identifying each format from scratch and reasoning through why some password-cracking attacks finish in seconds while others are deliberately designed to take much longer gave me a much deeper, hands-on understanding of key-derivation functions and the real-world tradeoffs behind password security.
